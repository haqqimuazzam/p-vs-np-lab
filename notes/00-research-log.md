# Research Log

## 2026-09-18 — Initialization

Repository created for rigorous P vs NP research.

Investigated:
1. Direct SAT diagonalization: finite candidate exclusion works; arbitrary literal self-reference does not follow from recursion theorems.
2. Kannan-style circuit lower bounds: fixed-exponent lower bounds do not automatically combine into one language defeating every polynomial exponent.
3. Proof complexity: P = NP implies NP = coNP; NP = coNP has the Cook-Reckhow proof-system characterization; restricted proof lower bounds do not automatically transfer to general Frege.

Current decision: do not claim a solution. Formalize PSRL and test whether the SAT-compressor property supplies enough structure to replace the invalid arbitrary fixed-point step.
